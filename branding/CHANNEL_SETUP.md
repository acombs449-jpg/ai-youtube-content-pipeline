## Quick setup (YouTube Studio → Customization)
Make sure you're acting as **The Daily Drop** channel (avatar menu → Switch
account → The Daily Drop), then go to Studio → Customization.

### Branding tab
- **Picture:** upload `branding\yt_avatar.png`
- **Banner image:** upload `branding\yt_banner.png`

### Basic info tab
- **Name:** The Daily Drop  (already set)
- **Handle:** @TheDailyDrop4You  (already set)
- **Description** (replace the current one — first 2 lines show everywhere):
  ```

  budgeting, saving, credit, and smart money habits explained in plain English.
  ```

## Biggest sub-converter: a channel trailer
- Upload the trailer video (`output\trailer_*.mp4`), set it Public.
- Customization → Layout → "Video spotlight" → set it as the trailer **for people
  who haven't subscribed**.
- Pin your best long-form as the **Featured video** for returning subscribers.

## Why this matters for the 1k goal
Shorts bring REACH but convert subs poorly. Viewers click your profile → the
banner + trailer + about either convert them or lose them. Long-form earns the
WATCH HOURS and trust that makes people subscribe. So: Shorts for discovery →
strong channel page → long-form for subs + hours.
