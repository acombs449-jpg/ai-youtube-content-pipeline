# Money Minute — Content Plan (road to 1,000 subs)

Two jobs: (1) playlists that chain videos into watch-hours, (2) a long-form
backlog so the channel looks established and earns subscribers. All topics are
educational only — no specific investment picks, no guaranteed returns.

## Playlists to create (YouTube Studio → Content → Playlists)
Order them on your homepage top-to-bottom. Each playlist auto-plays the next
video = more watch time = faster to 4,000 hours.

1. **Budgeting Basics** — "Take control of every dollar."
2. **Saving Money** — "Keep more of what you earn."
3. **Crush Your Debt** — "Get out of debt for good."
4. **Build Your Credit** — "Raise your score, unlock better rates."
5. **Investing for Beginners** — "Make your money work for you."
6. **Money Habits & Mindset** — "Think like someone who never goes broke."

## Launch checklist (look established on day one)
- [ ] Channel page set up (avatar, banner, about, trailer, handle) — see CHANNEL_SETUP.md
- [ ] Create the 6 playlists above (even empty — shows direction)
- [ ] Have 3-5 Shorts + 1-2 long-form already PUBLIC before promoting anywhere
- [ ] Trailer set as "for people who haven't subscribed"

## Cadence target
- Shorts: 1/day (automated, 4 PM) — discovery engine
- Long-form: 1-2/week (automated, Sat) — subs + watch hours
- Each long-form should reference/point to related Shorts and vice versa.

## How the agent uses this
The daily agent auto-researches fresh Short topics (and avoids repeats via
history.json). For long-form, you can let it auto-pick, or feed it a specific
backlog title when you want a particular video. Tag each upload into its playlist
in Studio (or we can automate playlist assignment later).
